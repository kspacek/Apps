Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how UDT s.r.o. ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Countdy mobile application (the "App"). Countdy is designed with a privacy-first approach: your countdowns live on your device — we run no servers and have no user accounts.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is UDT s.r.o., a company registered in the Czech Republic (D-U-N-S 88057446). Contact: support@udt.cz.

2. What Data We Collect

Your countdowns and personal data never reach our servers. The only information we receive is anonymous, opt-out usage analytics, described below. We never collect, transmit, store, or process any of the following:

  • Countdown titles, dates, notes, or categories you create
  • Photos you attach as countdown backgrounds
  • Calendar events — calendar access is read-only, processed entirely on your device, and used only to create the countdowns you explicitly pick
  • Personal identifiers, advertising IDs, or device fingerprints

Anonymous usage analytics. To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the App sends anonymous usage events (for example "app launched" or "countdown created") to Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service hosted in the European Union. These events contain no name, email, account, advertising identifier, or persistent device identifier, are not linked to your identity, and are never used for tracking or advertising. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → About → Share anonymous usage data; when off, nothing is sent.

3. Where Your Data Lives

On your device: All countdowns, photos, and settings are stored locally in the App's private container. Widgets and the Apple Watch app read the same on-device data.

Family sharing (optional): If you start or join a family, shared countdowns are stored in the family founder's personal Apple iCloud account via Apple CloudKit and are accessible only to the members they invite. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple; we have no access to it. Photos are never shared. Apple acts as a sub-processor under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). You can leave the family at any time in Settings → Family Sharing; the founder can stop sharing entirely, which removes shared countdowns for all members.

Share links: When you share a single countdown as a link, the countdown data travels inside the link itself. No server of ours is involved; whoever receives the link receives an independent copy.

4. In-App Purchases

Countdy offers a single one-time purchase (Countdy Unlimited) processed by Apple Inc. via the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information, credit card numbers, or Apple ID. Apple provides us only with anonymized purchase receipts to validate entitlements. Apple's privacy policy applies to all payment processing.

5. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, our legal basis under the GDPR for the limited processing described above is:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for processing your in-app purchase to deliver the Unlimited entitlement.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for ensuring the App functions correctly and for anonymous, opt-out usage analytics.

We do not rely on consent because we do not collect personal data on our servers.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and similar laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act), you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold any personal data on our servers, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device:

  • Erase your data: Delete countdowns in the App, or delete the App.
  • Erase shared data: Leave the family in Settings → Family Sharing (your own countdowns stay on your device as personal ones); the family founder can stop sharing for everyone.
  • Turn off analytics: Settings → About → Share anonymous usage data.
  • Contact us: For any other request, write to support@udt.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the Czech Republic: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.cz).

7. Children

Countdy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. As we collect no personal data on our servers at all, this restriction is satisfied by design.

8. International Transfers

Because we do not collect data on our servers, no international transfers occur on our part. Anonymous analytics are hosted by Aptabase in the European Union. If you use family sharing, Apple may store the encrypted data in data centers located outside your country, subject to Apple's privacy policy and Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version will always be available at countdy.app/privacy. Material changes will be announced in App Store release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@udt.cz.
