Why no loops matters at 3am

Most sleep sound apps record a 30-second audio clip and loop it. Your brain detects the pattern — especially in quiet, half-awake states. SilkSleep uses real-time DSP synthesis: the audio is generated sample-by-sample using filtered noise algorithms. There is no clip. There is no loop. There is nothing to detect.

Gaussian noise → bandpass filters → envelope modulation → DAC output
