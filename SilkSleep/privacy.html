Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026 · Version 1.0 · UDT s.r.o.

Short version SilkSleep generates all sound on your device in real time. We collect zero data. No analytics, no crash reporters sending data to us, no accounts, no servers. Your sleep is private by design.

1. Data Collection

SilkSleep does not collect any personal data. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app. Everything runs on-device.

We do not collect:

  • Sound preferences, preset names, or session durations
  • Names, email addresses, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers, IDFA, IP addresses, or analytics events
  • Health or sleep data (SilkSleep does not access HealthKit)
  • Crash reports sent to our servers
  • Any third-party SDK telemetry (we use zero third-party SDKs)

2. Data Storage

All preferences, presets, and settings you configure in SilkSleep are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data is in your app's private sandbox, inaccessible to other apps or to us.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your presets and settings are replicated to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. UDT s.r.o. has zero access to your iCloud container. Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy applies. You can disable sync any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

3. Audio Generation

SilkSleep generates audio entirely in real time using DSP algorithms running on your device's CPU. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted. The sound generation process has no network component.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. We receive a transaction receipt to verify your purchase. We do not receive payment card details, billing addresses, or Apple ID credentials.

5. GDPR (European Users)

UDT s.r.o. is registered in the Czech Republic, an EU member state. Because we collect no personal data, we do not act as a data controller under the GDPR. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide required disclosures.

Because data is stored only on your device, you exercise data rights (access, erasure) directly by deleting the app. There is no server-side profile.

6. CCPA (California Users)

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising. No personal information is collected by us in the first place.

7. Children's Privacy

SilkSleep is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The app contains no advertising and no third-party data collection.

8. Data Deletion

Deleting SilkSleep from your device removes all local data. If iCloud sync was enabled, delete the SilkSleep iCloud data via iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → SilkSleep.

9. Changes

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and note the change in App Store update notes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@udt.cz
