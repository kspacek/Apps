Frequently Asked Questions

Why does SilkSleep sound different from other sleep apps?

SilkSleep generates audio in real time using DSP (digital signal processing) algorithms — it never plays a pre-recorded file. Other apps loop a short recording and your brain can detect the repetition. With SilkSleep there's no loop to detect, ever.

How do I set a sleep timer so the app stops automatically?

Tap the timer icon in the main screen. Choose a duration (15, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes, or custom). SilkSleep will gently fade out and stop — no jarring silence cutoff.

Can I use SilkSleep with the screen off?

Yes. SilkSleep uses iOS background audio mode to keep playing when your phone's screen locks. The audio generation continues at full quality with no interruption.

How do I restore my Full Unlock purchase on a new device?

Open SilkSleep → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. The restore is instant and free.

How do I add SilkSleep to my Apple Watch?

Full Unlock includes the Apple Watch app. After purchase, open the Watch app on your iPhone → My Watch → scroll to SilkSleep → toggle "Show App on Apple Watch". Controls appear on your watch within a minute.

The sound cuts out when I receive a call or notification. Is that normal?

iOS will pause audio for incoming phone calls. SilkSleep will resume after the call. For notifications, enable "Do Not Disturb" or "Focus: Sleep" mode to avoid interruptions during sessions.

Does SilkSleep drain my battery overnight?

SilkSleep's DSP engine is optimised for long sessions. In our testing, a full 8-hour overnight session uses approximately 8–12% battery on iPhone 15 (screen off, Bluetooth earbuds connected). Plug in overnight if concerned.

Can I mix multiple sounds together?

Full Unlock includes a layer mixer — combine up to 3 generators simultaneously (e.g., Rain + Fan + White Noise) and save the mix as a named preset.

I want a refund. How?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund directly from Apple.

Does SilkSleep work without Wi-Fi or mobile data?

Completely. SilkSleep requires zero network access. Sound is generated on your device — no servers, no streaming, no data needed.
