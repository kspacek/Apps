Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a Countdy widget to my Home Screen?

Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen → tap "+" in the top-left → search for "Countdy" → choose a size and tap "Add Widget". Then tap the widget to select which countdown to display.

My widget isn't updating. What do I do?

Widgets refresh on iOS's schedule. Try removing and re-adding the widget. If the problem persists, restart your iPhone and check that Background App Refresh is enabled for Countdy in iOS Settings.

I bought Countdy Lifetime on another device. How do I restore?

Tap the "Restore Purchases" button in Countdy Settings. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do I add Countdy to my Apple Watch?

Countdy Lifetime includes the Apple Watch app. After purchasing, open the Watch app on your iPhone → My Watch → scroll to Countdy → toggle "Show App on Apple Watch". Then add a Countdy complication to your watch face.

Can I use Countdy on multiple iPhones or iPads?

Yes. Countdy Lifetime is tied to your Apple ID, not a single device. Sign in with the same Apple ID and restore your purchase on any device.

How do recurring countdowns work?

When a recurring countdown reaches zero, Countdy automatically advances it to the next occurrence (next year for yearly, next month for monthly, etc.). You'll see both the current and upcoming occurrences in the app.

I want a refund. How do I request one?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund directly from Apple. We don't process refunds ourselves.

Does Countdy work offline?

Yes, completely. Countdy requires no internet connection. All countdown calculations happen on-device.

Can I share a countdown with someone who doesn't have Countdy?

When you share a countdown link, the recipient needs Countdy installed to open it. If they don't have the app, they'll be directed to the App Store. The countdown opens instantly — no account required.

Will there be more themes in the future?

Yes! We plan to add new themes with major iOS updates. Countdy Lifetime holders get all future themes at no extra cost.
