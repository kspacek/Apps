Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026 · Version 1.0 · UDT s.r.o.

Short version Countdy stores all your countdown data on your device. We do not collect, see, or have access to any of your countdowns, dates, or personal information. Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero servers on our end.

1. Data Collection

Countdy does not collect any personal data. We do not operate backend servers that receive or store your information. The app functions entirely on-device.

We do not collect:

  • Countdown names, dates, photos, or categories
  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or analytics events
  • Purchase history beyond what Apple provides to us via StoreKit
  • Crash reports sent to our servers (crashes are surfaced via Apple's Xcode Organizer only)

2. Data Storage

All data you enter in Countdy is stored locally on your device using SwiftData (Apple's framework built on SQLite). The data file resides in your app's private sandbox, inaccessible to other apps or to us.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your countdown data is replicated to your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. UDT s.r.o. has zero access to your CloudKit container. Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy governs that storage. You can disable sync at any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

3. Shared Countdowns

When you share a countdown via the "Share via link" feature, a compact representation of your countdown (name, target date, theme) is encoded directly in the URL. No data passes through our servers — the URL is generated on your device and delivered peer-to-peer via your chosen sharing method (Messages, AirDrop, etc.).

4. Calendar Access

If you use the Calendar import feature, Countdy requests read-only access to your Calendar events. We only read events you explicitly select within the app. We do not read, store, or transmit past events or your full calendar.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only a transaction receipt to verify your purchase status. We do not receive your payment card details or billing address.

6. GDPR (European Users)

UDT s.r.o. is registered in the Czech Republic, an EU member state. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not act as a data controller under the GDPR in respect of your countdown data. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide the required disclosures.

As Countdy stores data only on your device, you exercise your data rights (access, rectification, erasure) directly through the app or by deleting the app.

7. CCPA (California Users)

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. In Countdy's case, no personal information is collected by us in the first place.

8. Children's Privacy

Countdy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Countdy does not contain advertising or third-party SDKs.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data exists only on your device (and optionally in your iCloud account). To delete all Countdy data, delete the app from your device. If iCloud sync was enabled, also remove the Countdy iCloud data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Countdy.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and post a notice in the App Store update notes. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@udt.cz. We're a small team and respond within 48 hours.
